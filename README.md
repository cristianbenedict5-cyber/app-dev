# ⚔️ Ang Panday (1980)

## Overview

**Ang Panday** is a 1980 Filipino fantasy-action film starring Fernando Poe Jr. as Flavio, a blacksmith who forges a magical weapon from a meteorite. He uses his enchanted sword to fight evil forces and protect his people from the tyrant Lizardo.

> "Ang tunay na lakas ay nagmumula sa tapang at kabutihan."

## Quick Facts

1. **Release Year:** 1980
2. **Director:** Ronwaldo Reyes (Fernando Poe Jr.)
3. **Starring:** Fernando Poe Jr.
4. **Genre:** Fantasy / Action / Adventure
5. **Country:** Philippines

## Featured Characters

- **Flavio:** The blacksmith who becomes the legendary Panday.
- **Lizardo:** The main villain who rules through fear.
- **Monica:** Flavio's companion.
- **Tata Temio:** The older Panday who guides Flavio.
- **Lando:** One of Flavio's companions.

## Personal Reflection

**What did I learn from Ang Panday?**

The movie teaches us that being a hero is not about having extraordinary powers or a magical weapon. It is about having courage, standing up for what is right, and protecting innocent people.

**Final Thought:** A true hero is not defined by the weapon he carries, but by the courage and goodness in his heart.
